✨ Major Historical & Natural Sights in Lualaba

🌊 Nzilo Dam & Reservoir Lake

Engineered on the upper runs of the Lualaba River (Congo River source), located roughly 30 km north of Kolwezi. A panoramic industrial landscape, widely popular for engineering field trips and weekend boat assessments.

⛏️ Open-Pit Geologic Formations

Kolwezi rests on the world's richest sediment strata. Authorized municipal guided tours provide access to observation decks displaying massive industrial open-pit operations, reflecting unparalleled earth moving operations.

🎨 Manika Cultural Artisanal Markets

Witness authentic local processing of raw materials. Explore handcrafted malachite sculptures, custom Katangan copper art work, local metalsmith networks, and discover the culinary legacy of the Katanga region.
