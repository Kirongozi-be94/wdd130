✨ Major Tourist Attractions in & around Kolwezi

🌊 Nzilo Dam & Lake

Located on the Lualaba River (Upper Congo), about 30 km north of Kolwezi. A breathtaking engineering marvel perfect for scenic views, water assessment, and weekend getaways.

⛏️ Katebi Artisan & Open-Pit Panoramas

Kolwezi is world-famous for its unique geology. Safe guided tours can offer viewpoint access to some of the most impressive and massive industrial mining excavations on Earth.

🎨 Manika Cultural Markets

Explore the authentic local culture! Discover traditional Katangan copper art, local handcrafts, malachite jewelry, and enjoy the legendary hospitality and gastronomy of Lualaba.
